The KOHLER 22169-AF Forte Multifunction Showerhead is built around Kohler's long-running Forte platform, a line the company has sold in various finishes for over a decade. The 22169-AF specifically pairs a multifunction spray face with an ABS plastic body finished in chrome plating, which keeps the unit light enough for a straightforward swap onto a standard shower arm. Flow is capped at 2.5 gallons per minute, the current ceiling under US federal water-efficiency rules, so you are not trading water savings for this fixture's extra spray modes.

Verified owner reviews for this Kohler 22169-AF Forte Multifunction Showerhead review center on two recurring themes: the spray face's resistance to the mineral scale that clogs cheaper showerheads over time, and the convenience of having several spray patterns on one fixed head rather than juggling a separate handheld attachment. The most common complaint in that same feedback points to price. Buyers comparing it against options like the SR SUN RISE Shower Faucet at $169.99 or the Delta Modern 14 Series Square at $142.08 note that the Forte costs more than several alternatives with similar flow ratings, and Kohler's brand name accounts for part of that premium.

What We Liked

The clearest strength we found researching this Kohler 22169-AF Forte Multifunction Showerhead review is consistency. Owners across a range of verified reviews describe the spray face holding up over months of regular use without the clogging that plagues cheaper plastic showerheads in hard-water areas. That tracks with Kohler's mineral-resistant coating, designed specifically to resist buildup on the nozzle holes.

Reviewers also point to the straightforward installation process on a standard shower arm, since the 22169-AF uses a conventional threaded connection rather than a proprietary mount. That matters if you are replacing an existing showerhead and want to avoid buying extra adapters.

The chrome finish reads as a close match to other Kohler bathroom hardware, which several owners cite as a reason they chose this model when the rest of their fixtures were already Kohler branded.

What Could Be Better

The most consistent criticism in the owner reviews we researched is price relative to flow performance. At $159.90, the Forte costs more than the Delta Modern 14 Series Square ($142.08) and sits close to the SR SUN RISE Shower Faucet ($169.99), without a published star rating we could point to as evidence it outperforms either on raw water pressure.

A smaller but recurring complaint involves the plastic body. Kohler finishes the ABS shell in chrome plating, and while that keeps the unit light, a handful of reviewers mention it feels less substantial in hand than fully metal showerheads in the same price range.
