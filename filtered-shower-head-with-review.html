Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Vatex Filtered Shower Head with Handheld worth it compared to the SR SUN RISE?
At $25.99 the Vatex costs about a dollar more than the SR SUN RISE, and both list a multi-stage filter cartridge and a handheld wand. The SR SUN RISE has 6,420 verified ratings behind it at 4.5 stars, while the Vatex is new enough that it has not built up that review history yet, so you are weighing a longer track record against a higher advertised pressure boost.
How long do filter cartridges last in a filtered shower head?
Listings for this category typically point to a few months of daily use per cartridge before the chlorine and sediment reduction drops off, and several of the models in this filtered shower head with review comparison ship with spare cartridges, such as the FunnyAir's three included filters, to cover the first year without a separate purchase.
Do filtered shower heads actually remove chlorine and hard water minerals?
The cartridges in these shower heads use KDF-55, activated carbon, or calcium sulfite media, which manufacturers say reduces chlorine and some heavy metals before the water reaches the nozzles. None of these filters soften water the way a whole-house softener does, so expect milder skin and hair effects rather than a fix for scale buildup on tile or fixtures.
