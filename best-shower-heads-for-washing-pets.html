Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best shower head for washing pets?
Based on our comparison of price, spray modes, and verified owner reviews, the Filtered Shower Head with Handheld stands out at $19.99 with a 4.9-star rating across 87 reviews. It pairs a detachable handheld wand with a long hose, which makes it easier to rinse a dog or cat without chasing them around the tub.
Do you need a special shower head to wash a dog?
You do not need a specialty pet shower head, but a handheld model with a long hose and multiple spray settings makes the job easier than a fixed head. Owners consistently report that a detachable wand gives them better control around a dog's face, paws, and belly than a stationary shower head bolted to the wall.
Is a filtered shower head worth it for pet baths?
A filtered shower head is worth considering if your home has hard water, since filtered models reduce chlorine and mineral buildup that can dry out a pet's skin and coat over repeated washes. Several of the shower heads in this roundup, including the FEELSO and PWERAN models, build filtration into the handle.
How much water pressure do you need to wash a dog or cat?
Most pets do not need high water pressure, and a gentle to medium spray setting is usually safer for their skin and ears. That said, models with multiple spray modes, like the two AquaCare High Pressure options in this roundup, let you dial up pressure when you need to rinse thick shampoo out of a heavy coat.
How do you rinse a dog without a handheld shower head?
Without a handheld head, most owners resort to filling a cup or pitcher and pouring water over the pet, which is slower and harder to control around the face and ears. A handheld shower head with a hose, like every pick in this guide, replaces that workaround with a continuous, adjustable stream.
