What Could Be Better

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the MDhair High Pressure 20 actually soften hard water?

It filters water rather than softening it in the technical sense. The 20-stage cartridge uses KDF-55 and activated carbon to reduce chlorine, heavy metals and some microplastics, which is what NSF-42 certification covers. It will not remove the calcium and magnesium minerals that cause scale buildup the way a whole-house softener does.

Will the MDhair filter reduce my water pressure?

MDhair ships two flow restrictors, a 2.5 GPM and a 1.8 GPM option, so you can choose the setting that matches your existing plumbing. Filtered shower heads generally trade a small amount of pressure for filtration, though owners report the drop is minor once the correct restrictor is installed.

How often do you need to replace the MDhair filter cartridge?

MDhair recommends replacing the 20-stage cartridge roughly every two to three months with regular use, similar to comparable KDF-55 filters on the market. Households on harder municipal water may need to swap it sooner, since heavier mineral content saturates the media faster.
