Is the ENGA Dual Rain LED Shower worth $569.99?

It depends on what you already have installed. If you only want a bigger rain head, the cheaper alternatives cover that. If you want the LED feature and a two-piece system in one box, the ENGA is the only option here that offers it.

How does the ENGA Dual Rain LED Shower compare to the Dasan Thermostatic Dual Shower Head?

Both pair a large rain head with a second shower unit and share the same ABS-and-chrome build common at this price point, but the ENGA adds LED lighting instead of a thermostatic valve. The ENGA also costs about $90 less than the Dasan. Choose the ENGA if lighting matters more to you than built-in temperature control, and choose the Dasan if you would rather set a fixed water temperature.

What size is the ENGA Dual Rain LED shower head?

The ENGA measures 14 by 20 inches, noticeably larger than the 12 inch Veken and the smaller SparkPod head covered in this comparison. A rain plate that size covers more of your shoulders and back at once, but it also needs enough ceiling or wall clearance to mount, so measure your shower stall before you buy.
