Frequently Asked Questions

Is the HOMEDEC double shower heads system worth the $1,050.98 price?

It depends on what you are replacing. The HOMEDEC bundles two shower heads, the arm, and the mixing hardware into one purchase, so the price covers a full system rather than a single fixture. Buyers who need a complete swap tend to find that easier to justify than buyers who want only a new head on an existing arm.

How does the HOMEDEC compare to the Delta Modern 14 Series Square?

The Delta Modern 14 Series Square lists at $636.01, well under the HOMEDEC's $1,050.98, and carries a brand with a long track record in US plumbing fixtures. The HOMEDEC counters with two heads instead of one and a full-system bundle, so the choice comes down to whether you need dual output or a single, trusted fixture at a lower cost.

Do double shower heads reduce water pressure?

Running two heads from the same supply line can lower pressure at each head compared to a single-head setup, since the same water volume now splits two ways. Homes with strong incoming pressure notice this less than homes that already struggle with weak flow, so your existing water pressure matters more than the shower head you choose.
