What does CUPC certification mean on a shower head like the Hibbent?

CUPC is a dual certification mark used across the US and Canada to confirm a plumbing fixture meets recognized safety and performance codes. For the Hibbent All Metal CUPC Certified shower head, that means it has passed third-party testing for leaks, pressure handling, and material safety, which matters if your local building code or contractor requires certified fixtures during a renovation or inspection.

Is the Hibbent All Metal CUPC Certified shower head actually all metal?

The product name advertises an all-metal build, but the spec sheet Hibbent publishes lists ABS plastic with a chrome-plated finish as the material.
