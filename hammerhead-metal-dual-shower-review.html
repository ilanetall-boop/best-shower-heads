Frequently Asked Questions

Is the HammerHead Metal Dual Shower Head made of solid metal?
No. This hammerhead metal dual shower review found the body is built from ABS plastic finished with chrome plating, not a solid-metal shell. The plating gives it the metal look and the added scratch resistance owners mention in verified reviews.
What flow rate does the HammerHead Metal Dual Shower Head run at?
It runs at 2.5 GPM, the ceiling US federal water-efficiency rules set for shower heads. That rate stays the same whether you use one spray setting or switch to the other, since GPM measures total flow through the head rather than the pattern you choose.
How does the HammerHead compare to the SR SUN RISE and Jolie alternatives in this review?
The HammerHead costs $179.95 and focuses on a dual-spray head alone. The two SR SUN RISE options, at $149.99 and $188.99, are complete faucet systems, and the Jolie Filtered Showerhead, at $169.00, adds water filtration instead of a second spray pattern. Which one fits depends on whether you are replacing only the head or the whole faucet setup.
