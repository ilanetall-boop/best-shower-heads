Is the JetsBath Luxury Thermostatic Rain system worth the price?

At $649.68, the JetsBath Luxury Thermostatic Rain system costs more than a standard shower head because it adds a thermostatic valve that locks in water temperature and a 22 inch rain head in a single chrome-plated ABS unit. What does the thermostatic valve on the JetsBath system actually do?

A thermostatic valve blends hot and cold water internally and holds the output at a set temperature, so a sudden pressure change elsewhere in the house, like a toilet flushing, does not send a scalding or cold surge through the shower head. This is the core feature that separates the JetsBath Luxury Thermostatic Rain system from a standard fixed rain head.
