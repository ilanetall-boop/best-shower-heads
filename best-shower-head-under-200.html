Frequently Asked Questions

Is a $200 shower head better than a $20 one?

Usually not by much. Our $19.99 top pick, the VMASSTONE waterfall head, outsprayed models at several times its price. Past $80 you pay for materials and lifespan, like the solid metal HammerHead builds, rather than for a stronger or wider spray.

Do all shower heads under $200 fit a standard shower arm?

All seven picks in this guide thread onto a standard US half-inch shower arm. Wrap the threads with plumber's tape, hand-tighten, and check for drips. None of them require a plumber or special tools.

Is 2.5 GPM good for a shower head?

2.5 gallons per minute is the maximum flow rate US federal law allows for shower heads, so it is the strongest option you can buy. The HammerHead Solid Metal 12 and the BRIGHT SHOWERS head in this guide both ship at that 2.5 GPM standard.

Do metal shower heads last longer than plastic ones?

Metal bodies resist cross-threading, survive scrubbing that wears through chrome plating, and keep their finish longer. That said, ABS plastic heads like the AquaDance routinely last for years, so the metal premium buys durability insurance more than a performance gain.

Which pick should I buy if I want the simplest choice?

Buy the VMASSTONE waterfall head. At $19.99 with a 9-inch face and strong pressure, it is the best shower head under 200 dollars for most bathrooms, and picking VMASSTONE leaves $180 of your budget untouched.
