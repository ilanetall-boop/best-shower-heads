Frequently Asked Questions

What is the ideal shower head height?
Most U.S. plumbing codes and builders install shower arms around 80 inches from the floor, roughly 6 to 8 inches above the tallest household member's head. Ideal shower head height comes down to coverage: a wider spray face, like the 11.8-inch Veken in this comparison, tolerates more variance from that 80-inch benchmark than a narrow 3-inch head does.
Can I fix a bad shower head height without replacing the plumbing?
Yes. Swapping in a wider spray-face head, like several of the picks above, spreads water across more vertical distance from the same fixed point. A handheld model with a hose, such as the Moen Engage Magnetix, goes further and lets you hold the spray at whatever height you actually need.
Does flow rate matter as much as spray face size for shower head height problems?
Both matter, but they solve different parts of the problem. Spray face size affects how much vertical area a fixed head covers, and flow rate, measured in gallons per minute, affects how strong the water feels once it reaches you. The BRIGHT SHOWERS pick above runs at the U.S. federal maximum of 2.5 GPM, which helps when weak pressure compounds a height mismatch, while the wider-faced Veken and Tudoccy address coverage more directly.
